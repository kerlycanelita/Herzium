# Herzium 1.10.6

Separate Fabric builds for Minecraft **1.21.10, 1.21.11, 26.1, 26.1.1, 26.1.2,
26.2 and 26.3**. Choose the JAR matching your exact game version.

- **Your click uses the item you were holding.** With the Herzium order, a
  hotbar key pressed right after a use or attack click, in the same tick, could
  change the item that click used: nexus, use, glowstone placed glowstone. That
  key now applies on the next tick instead, so the click keeps its item.
- The hotbar preview no longer turns itself off when another mod, such as KoHs
  Anchor's, or the server picks your slot.
- The Vanilla and Vanilla reversed orders are unchanged.

Requires **Fabric Loader 0.19.3+**. Minecraft 1.21.x uses **Java 21**; Minecraft
26.x uses **Java 25**. Install **Fabric API** for localized mod text and
**Mod Menu** to reach the settings button. Client-side only.

See the [1.10.6 audit](../audits/AUDIT-1.10.6-action-boundary.md) for the
laboratory results.
